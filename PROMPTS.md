# Prompt Library

## Color / Instagram style

**Cinematic**

Make this image look cinematic with controlled contrast, warm highlights, cool shadows, subtle film grain, and natural skin tones. Preserve the original composition and subjects.

**Vintage**

Give this image a tasteful vintage film look with slightly faded colors, gentle contrast, subtle grain, and warm analog character. Do not change the subject or composition.

**Black and white**

Convert this image into a high-quality black-and-white editorial photograph with rich tonal separation, detailed shadows, and natural skin texture.

**Warm**

Give the image a warm, inviting color grade with golden highlights and natural skin tones. Keep all objects and people unchanged.

## Environment

**Sunset**

Change the scene lighting to a realistic golden-hour sunset with warm directional light and believable shadows. Preserve the subject and scene geometry.

**Rainy**

Make the scene feel like a realistic rainy evening with wet surfaces, soft reflections, and overcast lighting, while preserving the main subject.

## Portrait

**Portrait blur**

Create a natural shallow-depth-of-field effect. Keep the main person and facial details sharp while softly blurring the background.

**Studio portrait**

Transform the lighting into polished studio portrait lighting with soft key light, gentle fill, controlled highlights, and a clean professional look. Preserve identity and clothing.
